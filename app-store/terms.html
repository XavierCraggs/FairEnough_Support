Houses & shared data

How shared houses work

FairEnough is built around houses, not individuals in isolation. When you join a house, your name, the chores you're assigned or complete, your part of bill splits and settlements, and anything you post to the house's shared messenger or calendar become visible to the other members of that house. This is how the app works, not an accident of the design — housemates need to see each other's contributions for the fairness scoring and bill splitting to mean anything.

Leaving a house stops new activity from being shared with it, but historical records — past chore completions, transactions, and messages — may remain visible to members who were part of the house at the time, for the sake of an accurate shared record.

A free house can have up to 8 members. This limit may change over time.

Subscriptions

House Pass

House Pass is an optional paid upgrade, currently priced at $3.99 AUD per house per month. It's billed to the Apple ID of whoever purchases it, through Apple's App Store subscription system.

  • Subscriptions automatically renew each billing period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends
  • Your Apple ID account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the current period
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions — we don't process cancellations directly, as we don't have the ability to
  • Prices are shown in your local currency at checkout and may change; we'll give notice of any price change before it applies to you
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under their own refund policies, not by us directly
